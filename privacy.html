OW Performance – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

OW Performance is a personal training app used by its developer only. It is not distributed publicly.

What data the app uses

  • WHOOP (with your permission via WHOOP login): recovery, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, strain, workouts, basic profile and body measurements.
  • COROS (optional, with your permission via COROS login): activities, training load, sleep and HRV.
  • Data you enter yourself: goals, training plan, daily check-ins, supplements.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored only on your own iPhone. There is no server and no database operated by the developer. Login tokens and API keys are kept in the iOS Keychain.

Who the data is shared with

  • Data is fetched directly from WHOOP and COROS to your phone.
  • When you use the AI coach, the relevant training and recovery data is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response, under your own API key.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

Disconnect WHOOP/COROS in the app's settings and delete the app to remove all data. You can also revoke access at any time in your WHOOP or COROS account.
